# Exports

Ready-to-use renders of the screens in this system.

- `title-card.png`, `end-card.png`: 1920 by 1080, for the one-minute video.
- `readme-banner.png`: 2560 by 800 (the 1280 by 400 banner at 2x) for the top of the GitHub README.
- `app-icon-1024.png`: the app icon with transparent corners, for the favicon and home-screen icon.
- `big-screen-<scene>.png`: the five big-screen scenes at 1920 by 1080, for slides and the submission.
- `phone-<screen>.png`: the six Queen St phone screens at 3x, for slides and the submission.
