# RolloutBanner

The moment of herd immunity: the time to the last store, the proof, and a timeline where each store sits at its real time after the verdict.

**Props:** `version`; `stores` [{ store, t }]; `scams` [stopped, total]; `honest` [served, total].

- The timeline is to scale: a store at 1.6s sits four times as far down as one at 0.4s.
