# Using this design kit

Mahyar made this design system before the event (Nov 13, 2026). It was imported at 10:45 on Nov 14 as Bodega's style guide, and README.md at the repo root lists it under 'Built before the event'.

- Use tokens.css (or tokens.json), the fonts, and the logos, store marks and icons under assets/.
- README.md is the brand book. 1-screens.md walks through the screens beat by beat, and 2-build-spec.md lists every token, component and data shape. `components/<Name>/README.md` holds each component's guidelines.
- The components are built at the event in the app's React 19. The design system's React 18 bundle isn't part of this kit.
- There's no audience in the demo (decided Nov 13): only Mahyar and Kasra use the app. Skip the QR wall, QR cards and plates, and the live shopper count. On stage, the big screen sits beside a phone-size shop window.
